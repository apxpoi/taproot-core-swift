# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Provide actionable technical feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling or disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code of conduct and may restrict participation when necessary.
